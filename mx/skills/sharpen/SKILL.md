---
name: sharpen
description: Aim the task ahead, or the last answer or grilling round, at what the best expert would do and what the user is really after.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Before the work, or on what you just gave me: ask how the best expert in the field would approach it (their raw sources, their real methods, an outside check that the result is right), what the user is really after, and whether the task is over- or underspecified. Name the premise the current plan and its options share, and weigh the strongest option outside it. Loosen constraints that don't serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do, and leave the choices that are the user's to make open for them. Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger, and always keep one outside check that the result is right.

Then do the work, the framing unnarrated. On what you just gave me, that means re-deriving it in full: for each decision, what that expert would choose and why they would reject your pick; where you can name that reason, change the pick. Optimize for what the expert would judge correct, never for what satisfies the stated constraints most cheaply, and state every trade-off you take. A grilling round is re-done as a round, the design and its questions. The user has at most skimmed the original, so the result stands whole rather than as a diff against it.
