Think from the expert's perspective now. For every decision in what you just gave me, ask what the best expert in that field would concretely choose, argue for, and argue against, plus why they would reject your current pick; if you can name that reason, don't make that choice. Optimize for what that expert would judge correct, never for what satisfies the stated constraints most cheaply: "the user would probably accept it" is never a reason. State every trade-off you take; absorb none. Then re-state the result in full, re-derived, not defended; a grilling round gets re-done as a round, the design and its questions. The user has at most skimmed the original, so this is not feedback on any particular part of it.
