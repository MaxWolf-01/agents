The mx workflow as one map

Every piece of work rides one line from an intent to merged and leaves it only where its route has a step of its own; what a landing turns up comes back as new work, and the numbered stops are the only places the workflow asks for you.

  • main line: one ticket, one worker
  • fog: no brief can be written yet
  • rival shapes: several survive the draft
  • loose: a stranger would need no brief
  • child tickets: bigger than one worker
  • after a landing: back into the work
  • proposed: waits for your triage
  • a step an agent runs
  • routes share the step
  • you are asked here
  • only when a call is yours

what each stop asks of you

  1. 1
    grilling roundsanswer the questions by number, veto or amend any call by name; once none is left the build starts, and a call you have not ruled on stays marked
  2. 2
    side by sidepick among the rival shapes in front of their renders; the verdict goes into the ticket
  3. 3
    the review pageread the loose branch's diff; it merges on your word
  4. 4
    your rulingaccept, amend, redo or reject, on the calls the landing shows you; accept merges it
  5. 5
    a hingeruled alone before anything builds on it; with no call of yours in it, it merges on your standing yes
  6. 6
    your ruling, wholethe whole tree, on the calls the debrief puts to you; one child can still be amended, redone or rejected
  7. 7
    QAdrive what landed on your own machine while the rest of the frontier keeps running; what you find is filed open
  8. 8
    the review sessionat first drivable: dogfood the landed surface and dump raw findings; a reopened decision is grilled again
  9. 9
    triagewhen you start one: what agents filed on their own reading (a worker's punt, a review's finding), grouped by shared cause; you drop, merge or promote each
  10. 10
    the needs-me groupbetween sessions, every open ticket question and every build waiting in review, on the board
