# Name the spending categories

## Brief

Every imported entry lands under "Uncategorised". The categories are yours to name, a dozen of them
from a month of real statements, before any rule that assigns one is worth writing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A category list you have signed off, with two or three payees from your own statements under each.
