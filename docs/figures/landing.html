A landing: the worker's report, the calls that are yours, the ruling

The worker builds, tests, reviews its own branch and commits a report. The orchestrator reads the diff, renders the review page and sorts the build's calls. A build that carries no call yours to make merges on your standing yes, the accept you gave in advance, as far as its parent ticket's branch. One that carries some waits in review, and each of those calls is put to you with a show built around them.
