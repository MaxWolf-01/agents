mx workflow · grilling across sessions

Planning that spans sessions: the ticket carries the design, child tickets carry the open questions.

A grilling writes its design into the ticket round by round, so a later session reads the design from the file. A session that ends with questions open files them as child tickets that need you; a fresh session claims one, grills it, and lands the answer on the parent ticket. How many sessions the frontier takes is discovered, not declared. Once it is empty the ticket is cut, or built as it stands, and the calls you have not ruled on travel with it.
