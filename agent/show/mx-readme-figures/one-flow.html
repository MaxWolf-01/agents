mx workflow · one flow

One flow, three intensities, and where you come in.

Every piece of work enters the same way and is routed by one question. What differs per row is how much lands on disk and who builds. The build starts once no question is left for you and never waits for you to ratify a call; you rule on what was built. The orange chips are where your attention is asked for: the open questions of a round, and the demo and review page of every landing.
