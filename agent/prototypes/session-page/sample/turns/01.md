# Round 1, as text: two axes, and a page per session

## Questions

- [Q1] **One feature or two?** The words help in the terminal today; the page needs a prototype first.
  - (a) Two: the words land in `/mx:show` on their own, the page is its own feature. *my pick*
  - (b) One: the page is where most of the grid can render, so they ship together.
- [Q2] **What stays in the chat reply once a page exists?**
  - (a) The index: one line, the link, one line per question. *my pick*
  - (b) The full reply as today, the page rendering the same text better.
- [Q3] **One page per session, or per piece of work?** A session often spans several tickets or none.
  - (a) Per session, linking into the work's artifacts. *my pick*
  - (b) Per feature, ticket or branch, every session on it appending to one page.

## Details

Your six ways of explaining sit on two axes rather than a ladder: the level you look at (the concept, the code, the output) and whether you see the thing as it is or how it changed. A narrated example is still prose; a pasted run is output, so the line that matters is narrated against real, not abstract against concrete. The reply went to the terminal as text, the grid as a markdown table.
